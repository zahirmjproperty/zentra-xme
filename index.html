About the Project

THE INDUSTRIAL GATEWAY
OF NEGERI SEMBILAN

XME Business Park 2 is a freehold light industrial development by Sime Darby Property Berhad, located within the Nilai Impian township in Nilai, Negeri Sembilan — on the corridor between Negeri Sembilan and Selangor.

Phase 3B offers 59 factory units: 20 semi-detached units of 6,554 – 7,286 sq ft built-up and 39 linked units of 3,422 – 4,399 sq ft built-up. Every unit is designed around a triple-volume warehouse of at least 9 metres clear height, with an open-plan office on the first floor.

The land is light industrial with full infrastructure, and the developer states that it is converted. Development components, specifications and phasing are as set out in the developer's sales kit for Phase 3B.
